a letter, just for you ♡

Meri Aditi,
my majesty, my home.

My love,

I don't know whether something has been bothering you lately. Maybe I'm reading things wrong, and if I am, that's okay. I never want you to feel like you have to explain yourself just because I'm wondering. I only want you to know that if there is something on your mind, you don't have to carry it quietly just to protect me or keep everything looking okay.

You can bring me the things you don't know how to say yet. You can bring me a bad day, a messy thought, a fear that doesn't make sense out loud, or absolutely nothing at all. We can sit in silence. We can talk until the sky changes colour. We can take one tiny step and then another. You do not have to solve your whole world in one night, meri mutu.

You don't have to do everything alone. Please remember that, my majesty. Being strong doesn't mean never leaning on anyone. You are allowed to rest. You are allowed to need reassurance. You are allowed to be soft with me. I may not always have the perfect answer, but I will listen with my whole heart, ask what you need instead of assuming, and do my best to make you feel safe and loved.

Aditi, mero mutu, mero sabaithok—you mean more to me than I know how to fit into a letter. I love your smile, your little ways, your mind, the things you care about, the things that make you wonderfully you. I love you when you're glowing and when you feel like you have no light left to give. You never have to be the easiest version of yourself to deserve my love. You are already enough. You have always been enough.

Mero devi, sometimes I think I could spend a whole lifetime trying to show you what your existence means to me and still have more love left to explain. I would kiss the ground your feet walk on—not because you need to be put on a pedestal, but because even the ordinary places become special to me when they've been part of your day. If life is unkind to you, I want to be a place where kindness waits. If your heart gets tired, I want you to remember my arms are not a place you have to earn your way into.

I want the big things with you, yes, but I want the tiny things just as much: asking what we should eat, stealing a blanket, laughing at jokes that make no sense, seeing your face when you wake up, holding your hand while we do completely ordinary things. I want to learn you in every season. I want to be someone you can tell the truth to. I want our future home to sound like laughter and feel like peace—and I want you to know that your dreams matter just as much as mine.

And for the record, my queen, my diva, the prettiest girl in this entire ridiculous universe: no constellation could compete with you. Not one. The stars can keep their little sparkle; I've already found the one I look for. ✨

So, if something is wrong, you can tell me. If nothing is wrong and you just need a cuddle, that is also an excellent reason to come to me. No pressure, no test, no need to have the right words. Just you, being you, and me loving you through it.

Timilai aja maya garchhu, bholi pani maya garchhu, ra hami le banाउने harek bholi ma pani timilai nai rojchhu. I choose you in the grand, movie-worthy moments and in the tiny, unnoticed ones. I choose you with patience, with honesty, with tenderness, and with the hope that we keep growing together.

Come here, meri mutu. Let me hold you for a while. The world can wait a minute.

Timro buda, Naitik — Aditi ko buda, aja, bholi, sadai. ♡
